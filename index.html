About Dustin Luke Kay

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is a talent acquisition and recruiting executive with experience in mortgage banking, enterprise technology, healthcare, staffing and professional services.

His work spans executive search, recruiting operations, strategic sourcing and team leadership.

His career includes leadership and recruiting work with PennyMac, American Pacific Mortgage, Accenture, Inogen, JDA TSG and Metro Systems.

He focuses on recruiting systems that support measurable business growth, develop strong teams and improve the candidate experience.

Luke served in the United States Navy from 1996 to 2004. His service included an assignment at Naval Air Station Fallon and responsibilities in career counseling.
