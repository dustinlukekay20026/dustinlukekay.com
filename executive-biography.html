About Dustin Luke Kay

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is an experienced talent acquisition executive and strategic recruiting leader with more than three decades of experience across mortgage banking, enterprise technology, healthcare, staffing and professional services.

His career has encompassed executive recruiting, strategic sourcing, organizational growth, recruiting operations and the development of high-performing talent acquisition teams. His leadership approach emphasizes relationships, measurable results and long-term organizational success.

Professional Career Highlights

PennyMac — Talent Acquisition Leadership

At PennyMac, Luke helped build and lead a mortgage loan officer recruiting organization. His recruiting operation expanded from approximately 300 annual hires to 900, supported by a dedicated team of loan officer recruiters.

Recruiting improvements also reduced average time-to-fill from approximately 36–42 days to 28 days.

Accenture — Senior Technical Recruiting

At Accenture, Luke contributed to enterprise technology recruiting, completing 172 hires during the first two quarters of 2015, including 26 hires in a single month. His performance earned Top Recruiter Spotlight recognition.

American Pacific Mortgage — Strategic Growth

As a Strategic Growth Partner, Luke developed relationships with mortgage professionals and identified recruiting opportunities representing significant potential production volume.

His recruiting activity included 209 conversations, 94 prospective opportunity meetings and approximately $1.086 billion in potential mortgage production identified.

Recruiting Team Leadership

Throughout his career, Luke has held recruiting management and talent acquisition leadership positions, including responsibility for a regional recruiting organization comprising 47 recruiters.

Mortgage Recruiting Expertise

Luke specializes in identifying and engaging mortgage loan officers, branch managers and mortgage sales leaders. His experience includes strategic recruiting outreach, relationship development, recruiting pipeline management and supporting organizational growth.

His background combines hands-on mortgage recruiting experience with executive-level talent acquisition strategy.

United States Navy Service

Dustin Luke Kay served in the United States Navy from 1996 to 2004. His military background included service at Naval Air Station Fallon, home of the Navy's TOPGUN program, where he served in command career counseling.

His Navy experience helped shape a leadership philosophy centered on accountability, professional development, service and commitment to organizational excellence.
