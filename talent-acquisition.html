Executive Talent Acquisition Leadership

Dustin Luke Kay, professionally known as Luke Kay, is an experienced talent acquisition executive and recruiting leader with more than three decades of experience developing recruitment strategies, building high-performing teams, and helping organizations attract exceptional talent.

His career spans mortgage banking, technology consulting, healthcare, professional staffing, and military service, combining hands-on recruiting execution with enterprise-level leadership.

Recruiting Leadership Highlights

  • Led regional recruiting operations involving a team of 47 recruiters at Metro Systems.
  • Built and scaled loan officer talent acquisition operations at PennyMac, supporting annual hiring growth from approximately 300 to 900 hires.
  • Improved loan officer time-to-fill from approximately 36–42 days to 28 days.
  • Delivered 172 hires during the first two quarters of 2015 at Accenture, including 26 hires in a single month.
  • Earned recruiting performance recognition, including a Top Recruiter Spotlight at Accenture and Top Recruiter of the Year at Metro Systems.

Mortgage Banking & Strategic Growth

Luke has extensive experience recruiting mortgage loan officers, branch managers, and sales leaders. His approach emphasizes production performance, relationship development, leadership alignment, and long-term organizational growth.

At American Pacific Mortgage, he generated approximately 94 recruiting opportunity meetings from 209 conversations over a nine-month period, identifying prospective mortgage production opportunities totaling more than $1 billion in potential volume.

His mortgage recruiting experience includes talent acquisition leadership at PennyMac, strategic growth recruiting at American Pacific Mortgage, and recruiting initiatives focused on high-performing mortgage professionals.

Technology, Healthcare & Professional Staffing

Beyond mortgage banking, Luke has held recruiting and talent acquisition roles involving technology consulting, healthcare organizations, and professional staffing.

His experience includes Accenture, JDA TSG, Metro Systems, Inogen, and Premier Talent Solutions, with responsibilities spanning individual recruiting production, recruiting management, talent acquisition strategy, and organizational growth.

United States Navy Service

Luke served in the United States Navy from 1996 to 2004, including service at Naval Air Station Fallon, home of the Navy's TOPGUN program.

His military experience helped establish the foundation for his leadership approach, emphasizing accountability, discipline, teamwork, and commitment to excellence.
